# Gikomba Stall Analysis

**Name:** Daniel Nyongesa

**Cohort:** 1Mill Devs Cohort 2026

Wanjiku runs a fresh produce stall at Gikomba Market but lacks a clear picture of her sales performance, the products that need restocking, and which products have the strongest profit margins. Without these insights, she risks losing sales when popular items run out and wasting money by holding more stock than she needs. This analysis uses 14 days of sales and current inventory data to identify sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, classify sales days, and guide restocking and product decisions.

**How to run:** Open [stall_analysis.ipynb in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/dnyongesa244-pro/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb) and run all cells from top to bottom.

## Top three findings

1. Total sales were **KES 46,450** over 14 days, with sales increasing **10.66%** from week 1 to week 2.
2. Weekend average sales were **KES 4,900**, which is **82.5% higher** than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
3. **Tomatoes and Sukuma need restocking**; Avocado had the highest gross margin at **42.9%**.
